Cookie Policy

Last updated: April 22, 2026

This Policy explains how the website lumiwebagency.com, owned by FOP Yeryomina Maria Mykolaivna, uses cookie files and similar technologies.

1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. They help the site "remember" your interaction (e.g. language, data consent, session) and function correctly.

2. What cookies we use

Necessary (essential)

Without these cookies the website cannot function properly. They do not store personal information and do not require your consent.

  • Saving your cookie banner preference.
  • Maintaining user session.

Analytical

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with the website — which pages are more popular, where you came from, how long you spent on the site. Data is anonymous and aggregated.

  • Google Analytics — visit statistics.
  • Meta Pixel / Instagram — if connected.

Functional

Allow the website to remember your choices (language, interface settings) for a more convenient experience.

3. How to manage cookies

You can:

  • Accept or decline cookies via the banner that appears on your first visit.
  • Configure or delete cookies in your browser settings:
    • Google Chrome
    • Mozilla Firefox
    • Safari
    • Microsoft Edge

Please note: if you disable cookies, some website features may not work correctly.

4. Third-party cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services we use:

  • Google (analytics, fonts)
  • Instagram / Meta (social media integration)
  • Telegram (messenger button)

We do not control these cookies. Their detailed terms of use are described in the privacy policies of the respective services.

5. Changes to the Policy

We may update this Policy. The current version is always available on this page. We recommend reviewing this document periodically.
