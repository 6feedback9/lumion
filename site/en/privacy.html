Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how FOP Yeryomina Maria Mykolaivna (hereinafter — "we", "Lúmi Web Agency") collects, uses and protects the personal data of users of the website lumiwebagency.com.

2. What data we collect

During your interaction with the website, we may collect the following categories of data:

  • Data you provide voluntarily — name, phone number, email, message in the request form.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, operating system, date and time of visit, pages viewed.
  • Cookies — small text files that help the website function correctly (see our Cookie Policy for details).

3. Photos for AI glasses try-on

If you use the AI glasses try-on feature (the LumiOn widget) on one of our client brands' websites, the following additional rules apply to the photo you upload:

  • The photo is used solely to generate the image with the glasses — not for advertising, unrelated analytics, or training third-party models.
  • To generate the result, the photo is sent over a secure channel to a third-party AI provider (FASHN.ai), which technically processes the image.
  • The original uploaded photo is not stored on our servers — only the finished result (the photo with the glasses) is kept, to be shown in the brand's dashboard.
  • Before uploading a photo, you confirm your consent to this Policy.
  • You can request deletion of a stored result at any time by emailing us at agencytakeiteasy@gmail.com.

4. How we use your data

  • To process your requests and provide website development services.
  • To contact you regarding your project, agree on details and send commercial proposals.
  • To improve website performance and analyse its usage.
  • To fulfil legal obligations (accounting, reporting).

5. Legal basis for processing

We process your personal data on the basis of:

  • your consent (submitting the request form);
  • necessity to fulfil a contract to which you are a party;
  • legitimate interests (service improvement, security);
  • compliance with applicable Ukrainian legislation.

6. Who we share your data with

We do not sell, transfer or provide your personal data to third parties, except in the following cases:

  • Partner services (hosting, analytics, email distribution) — to the minimum extent required for website operation.
  • Government authorities — upon official request in cases provided by law.

7. Data retention period

We store your personal data for as long as necessary for the purposes of processing, but no longer than the periods provided by Ukrainian legislation. Generally:

  • Request form data — up to 3 years from the last interaction.
  • Contract data — in accordance with the requirements of the Tax Code of Ukraine.

8. Your rights

Under Ukrainian law on personal data protection, you have the right to:

  • know the sources of collection and the purpose of processing your data;
  • access your data;
  • request correction of inaccurate data;
  • request deletion of data (right to be forgotten);
  • withdraw consent to data processing;
  • file a complaint with the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights.

To exercise any of these rights, write to us at agencytakeiteasy@gmail.com.

9. Data security

We implement technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, loss, alteration or disclosure. All connections to the website are secured via HTTPS.

10. Changes to the Policy

We reserve the right to amend this Policy. The new version takes effect upon publication on this page. We recommend reviewing this document periodically.
