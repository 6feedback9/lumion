Privacy

What happens to the customer's photo

We handle customer photos carefully — here's what actually happens on the technical side.

🔒
Used only for the try-on
The photo is used only to generate the result — not for advertising, not for training third-party models.
🤝
Processed via an AI provider
The photo is sent over a secure channel to a third-party AI service that generates the image with the glasses.
🗑️
The original photo isn't stored
Only the finished result (with glasses) is kept for the brand's gallery — the customer's original photo is not stored.
✅
Consent at upload
Before uploading a photo, the customer sees a link to the privacy policy and confirms consent.
