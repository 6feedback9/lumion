Selling in Czechia and Poland: A Website Checklist for Ukrainian Businesses in 2026

Translating the site is not enough. Here is what to change so your website actually sells in Czechia and Poland.

1. A dedicated language version, not just a translation

Czech and Polish users search differently from a literal translation. Category names, headings and copy should match local queries. Each language needs its own URL (for example /cz/ or a separate domain).

2. hreflang and clean URLs

The hreflang tag tells Google which language version to show in which country. Without it, versions can compete with each other. Each version must point to all the others and to itself.

3. Currency and prices

Show prices in local currency (Czech koruna, Polish zloty) and VAT information. Prices in a foreign currency reduce trust and conversion.

4. Payments

In Poland, shoppers are used to BLIK and Przelewy24; in Czechia, to local bank payments and gateways such as GoPay or Comgate. Cards are needed too, but a local method noticeably reduces abandoned carts.

5. Delivery and returns

State timing, cost and carriers for each country. Return terms must follow EU distance-selling rules, including the 14-day right of withdrawal.

6. GDPR and cookies

A cookie banner with a real choice, a privacy policy and company details are a must when serving EU customers.

7. Local SEO

  • A separate keyword set for each country
  • Meta tags and headings in the local language
  • Google Search Console for each version
  • Reviews and mentions on local resources

FAQ

Do I need a separate website for Czechia or Poland?

Not necessarily a separate domain: you can run language versions on one site with hreflang, local currency and payments.

Which platform is best for entering EU markets?

For multilingual stores and international payments many choose Shopify; see our platform comparison.

Does a Ukrainian business need GDPR compliance?

If you collect data from EU users, you need a privacy policy, an honest cookie banner and a legal basis for processing.
