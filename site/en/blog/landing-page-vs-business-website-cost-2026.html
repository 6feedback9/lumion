Landing Page vs Business Website: Cost, Differences and What to Choose in 2026

A landing page and a business website are not the same thing. Here is the difference, the prices and when each works best.

What is the difference

A landing page is a single page with one goal: get the visitor to leave a request, buy or call. A business website has several pages (about, services, contacts) that explain who you are and what you do.

Approximate prices in 2026

  • Landing page on a builder: about $100-300
  • Landing page with custom design: about $300-800
  • Business website (3-7 pages): about $400-1200
  • Multi-page corporate site: from about $1200

When to choose a landing page

  • One service or product
  • Paid ads that lead to a specific offer
  • You need a fast launch to test an idea

When to choose a business website

  • Several services to present separately
  • Trust matters: cases, team, reviews
  • You plan SEO and need separate pages for different queries

What affects effectiveness

Loading speed, a clear first screen, social proof, a clear call to action and a good mobile version. See our cases: there are both landing pages and full websites.

FAQ

How much does a landing page cost?

Roughly $100-300 on a builder and $300-800 with custom design.

How much does a business website cost?

Usually $400-1200 for 3-7 pages with custom design and basic SEO.

Which is better for ads?

For paid ads to one offer, a landing page; for organic traffic and trust, a business website.
