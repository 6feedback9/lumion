How Much Does an Online Store Cost in 2026: Prices by Platform

From a few hundred to several thousand dollars: the difference is not random. Here is what makes up the cost and what to choose for your budget.

Short answer: approximate prices

These are market guidelines for 2026. The exact amount depends on the number of products, integrations and design.

  • Builder (Prom, Weblium, Horoshop template): about $100-400 for setup plus a monthly subscription
  • Shopify / Horoshop with custom design: about $700-2000
  • WooCommerce (WordPress): about $800-2500
  • Custom development: from about $3000

What the cost consists of

1. Platform and hosting

Shopify and Horoshop charge a monthly fee, but hosting, security and updates are included. WooCommerce is free but needs hosting and regular maintenance.

2. Design

A ready template is cheaper; a custom design gives higher conversion and a recognisable brand.

3. Catalog and content

Importing products from Excel/YML, filters, attributes, variants. The more products, the more work.

4. Integrations

Delivery services, payment gateways, CRM, accounting, Google Analytics and the Meta pixel. Each integration is separate work.

5. SEO foundation

Category structure, meta tags, structured data and speed are set at the start and are expensive to fix later.

Which platform to choose

For a limited budget, Horoshop or a builder. For scaling into EU markets, Shopify. For a large catalog with unusual logic, WooCommerce or custom. See the platform comparison. For a general website, read how much a website costs.

Hidden costs

  • Platform subscription and paid apps
  • Product photos and copy
  • Updates, backups, security
  • Advertising and SEO: a store without traffic does not sell

FAQ

How much does it cost to create an online store?

Roughly $100-400 on a builder to $700-2500 on Shopify, Horoshop or WooCommerce with custom design. Custom development starts from about $3000.

How long does it take to launch an online store?

A Shopify or Horoshop store usually takes 2-4 weeks; a custom one 6-8 weeks or more.

Do I need SEO right after launch?

Yes. Basic SEO is built in during development; promotion starts right after launch.
