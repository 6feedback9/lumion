How to Get into Google Discover and Increase Website Visibility in 2026

Google Discover brings traffic without search queries — the article finds its own readers. We break down what influences appearing in Discover and how to optimize content for this channel.

What is Google Discover and why it matters

Google Discover is a content recommendation feed in the Google mobile app and on google.com on mobile devices. Unlike search, the user isn't looking for anything — Google itself suggests content based on their interests and behavior.

For businesses this means: traffic without a search query. Someone reads an article about website redesign and sees your publication about design trends — with no search at all.

What influences appearing in Google Discover

1. Content quality and authority (E-E-A-T)

Google evaluates Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness. Content that demonstrates real experience is needed: case studies, specific numbers, authorship with name and photo.

2. Large quality image

Articles in Discover appear with a large horizontal image. Minimum 1200px wide. The image is what the user sees first. Without a quality image, chances are minimal.

3. Freshness and relevance

Discover favors new publications and content about current events and trends. Articles with a publication date rank better. Updating old articles can also give results.

4. Behavioral signals

If your content is read well in search (low bounce rate, long time on page) — Google shows it in Discover to a similar audience.

5. Core Web Vitals

Technical site quality is a mandatory requirement. A slow site won't get into Discover.

How to optimize content for Google Discover

  • Title 50–90 characters — specific, interesting, no clickbait
  • Photo 1200×628px minimum — quality, relevant, with max-image-preview: large
  • Publication date in markup — Schema.org Article with datePublished and dateModified
  • Authorship — name, photo, link to author page
  • Article structure — H2/H3 that answer specific questions
  • Internal links — link articles to each other

How to track traffic from Google Discover

Google Search Console → Performance → search type: Discover. There you can see which articles got impressions and clicks from Discover.

📊
Important: Discover traffic is uneven. An article can collect 10,000 views in a week and then stop. This is normal. The goal is to regularly publish quality content and increase chances of viral spikes.
