Do You Need a Website If You Have Instagram: 5 Reasons One Account Is Not Enough

Instagram brings clients, but it does not belong to you. Here is what your own website changes and when you really need one.

What Instagram does well

A fast start with no budget, visual presentation, live conversations with customers and simple Direct messages. For testing an idea it is often enough.

5 reasons to have your own website

1. You do not own the account

A ban, an algorithm change or a drop in reach can leave you without customers overnight. The website and domain are yours.

2. People find you on Google

Buyers search for "buy…", "order…", "price…". A page on your site can answer that query; an Instagram post almost never does.

3. Trust

A site with prices, delivery terms, reviews and contacts looks like a more serious business, especially to new and B2B customers.

4. Automation

Lead forms, payment, CRM and analytics are all visible and measurable. In Direct, every order is handled by hand.

5. Scale

As orders grow, handling them in a messenger becomes a bottleneck. An online store takes orders without you.

When you need a site now

  • You answer the same questions about prices and delivery every day
  • Customers ask "is there a website?"
  • You want to run ads and measure results
  • You are expanding to other cities or countries

Best option: website and Instagram together

Instagram is a showcase and a trust channel, the website collects leads and sales. See how much a landing page or business website costs and how much an online store costs.

FAQ

Can I run a business only on Instagram?

You can, especially at the start. But you depend on someone else's platform and have no SEO traffic or sales automation.

Which is better: Instagram or a website?

They complement each other: Instagram attracts and builds trust, the website collects leads and sales.

Where do I start if I only have Instagram?

With a simple landing page or business website with prices, contacts and a lead form; then an online store if needed.
