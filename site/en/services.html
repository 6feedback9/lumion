Process

How we work

So you never get lost in stages and always understand what's happening at each step.

01

Brief & strategy

We define goals, pages, logic, positioning and desired results.

02

Structure & wireframe

We build the site skeleton — solving business tasks first, then moving to design.

03

Design & content

We shape the visual presentation, copy, accents and block logic for the target audience.

04

Build and launch

Front-end, mobile version, forms, analytics and checks before launch. SEO and GEO are added separately.

Approach

Our approach

We don't build sites "by picture". First we understand how you sell, where you lose customers and what logic leads to results.

Deep business immersion

We analyse your product, goals, processes and audience — only then do we design the site.

Aesthetics + function

It's not just about the "wow" effect — it's about how the site guides the user to submit a request.

Transparent process

We work in stages, agree on decisions, stay focused on priorities and don't get lost in endless revision rounds.
